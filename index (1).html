01 — About

Background

I'm a recent Computer Engineering graduate from Chandigarh University with a strong foundation in data analysis, SQL, Python, and visualization tooling. My coursework and projects span database management, data cleaning, and business intelligence.

Most recently, I worked as a Data Analyst Intern at Zorontech, where I cleaned and optimized real production datasets, tuned SQL queries for reporting speed, and built Tableau dashboards that management actually used to make calls.

I like the unglamorous part of analytics — fixing broken columns, deduplicating records, deciding how to handle nulls — because that's the part that determines whether the chart on top of it can be trusted.
